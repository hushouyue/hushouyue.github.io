I am a Robotics MSE student at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), a research assistant in the [IMERSE Lab](https://imerse.lcsr.jhu.edu/) (advised by Axel Krieger), and an intern in surgical simulation at [Inner Logic](https://innerlogic.io/) (advised by Gašper Podobnik and Peter Kazanzides).

I design models that describe physical phenomena and predict what happens next. My work focuses on GPU physics simulation of soft tissue and surgical tools for tasks such as tissue manipulation, cutting, needle insertion and suturing. These simulations let a surgical robot anticipate how tissue will respond before it acts, generate synthetic data for training learning-based models, and serve as a testbed for medical devices and for evaluating surgical robot autonomy.

Before Hopkins, I received my B.Comp. (Honours, Distinction) with a minor in Mathematics from [Queen’s University](https://www.queensu.ca/), then worked in Canada as a software engineer and ROS developer. In 2025, I was a Google Summer of Code open-source developer with [PAL Robotics](https://pal-robotics.com/).

Outside the lab I fence sabre, paint and sculpt — see [interests](/interests/).
